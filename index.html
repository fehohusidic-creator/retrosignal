WHAT IS THIS?

Retrosignal is a YouTube Shorts channel about the golden age of games: how CRT televisions and light guns worked, consoles that failed, forgotten hidden gems, developer tricks and the latest retro gaming news. Every episode is animated pixel art, hosted by Telly, a very small TV with very big opinions.

Videos are animated pixel art. Narration is synthetic (AI voice); scripts are researched and written with AI assistance, and news episodes link their sources.

RETRO SIGNAL UPLOADER

The channel is produced with a small private tool, the Retro Signal Uploader. It runs only on the channel owner's own computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload finished videos to the Retrosignal channel. It is not offered to anyone else and does not access other people's accounts.
