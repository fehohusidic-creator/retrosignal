PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 29 October 2026

This policy covers the Retrosignal website and the Retro Signal Uploader, a private tool that the operator of the Retrosignal YouTube channel uses to upload videos to that channel.

WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

The operator of the Retrosignal YouTube channel. Contact: feho.husidic@gmail.com

THE UPLOADER APP

The Retro Signal Uploader runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it. With the owner's consent through Google's sign-in, it requests these Google permissions:

  • youtube.upload - to upload videos and their title, description and tags to the Retrosignal channel, and to schedule when they are published;
  • youtube.readonly - to read the channel name and the privacy/processing status of the videos the tool has uploaded, so it can confirm an upload worked.

The sign-in tokens issued by Google are stored in a local file on the owner's computer that only the owner's user account can read. The app has no server, does not collect data about viewers or other users, and does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone.

Video scripts are written with the help of an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API) from public news headlines and topic ideas. No data obtained from Google or YouTube is sent to that service.

GOOGLE API SERVICES

The Retro Signal Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data received from Google APIs is used only to provide the upload function described above and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

YouTube's own data handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

RETENTION AND DELETION

The tool keeps a local log of its uploads (video IDs, titles, upload times) on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are kept until access is revoked. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the local token file is then useless and can be deleted.

THIS WEBSITE

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; when you visit it, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). Links to YouTube lead to Google's services, where Google's privacy policy applies.

If you are in the EU/EEA, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data concerning you, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. To exercise these rights, use the contact above.
